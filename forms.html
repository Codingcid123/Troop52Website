Quick Overview

New scouts and adults need the applications below. Everyone also needs medical forms on file. The Troop 52 Code of Conduct is reviewed every year.

Where to send completed forms: Pacific Skyline (PACSKY) Council, Stanford District representative — Rand Mahoney

Youth Application

  1. Fill out the form online on a computer, then print it.
  2. Get signatures from parent/guardian and unit leader.
  3. Send the completed form to Rand Mahoney (PACSKY / Stanford District).

Open Youth Application (PDF)

Adult Application

  1. Fill out the form online on a computer, then print it.
  2. Get signatures from the unit leader and committee chair.
  3. Send the completed form to Rand Mahoney (PACSKY / Stanford District).

Open Adult Application (PDF)

Medical Forms

Required for every scout and every adult leader.

  • Part A — Informed consent, release agreement, and authorization
  • Part B1 / B2 — General information and health history
  • Part C — Pre-participation physical (medical professional). Only needed for activities longer than 72 hours (for example, summer camp or an extended backpacking trip).

Download medical forms (BSA AHM&R)

Troop 52 Code of Conduct

Reviewed and completed once each year by the scout and a parent or guardian.

  • Follow scout values, troop rules, and leader directions at meetings and events.
  • Treat others with respect and help keep the troop safe.
  • Ask a leader if you are unsure about behavior or expectations.
