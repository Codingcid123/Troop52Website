Welcome to Troop 52

Troop 52 is a friendly Boy Scout troop where scouts build outdoor skills, confidence, and leadership. We learn by doing: camping, hiking, helping our community, and supporting each other as a team.

Our troop in the annual May Fete Parade

Quick Look at Troop Life

Outdoor Skills

Learn tents, camp cooking, trail planning, knots, and first aid.

Leadership

Practice responsibility through patrol roles and troop teamwork.

Service

Give back through cleanups, volunteering, and local support events.
